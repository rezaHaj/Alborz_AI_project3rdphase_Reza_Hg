# NeoScreenBot – Quick Guide

## Sampling
- Initial sampling window: **day 3–5 of life**.

## CH (TSH-only)
- **TSH < 5 mU/L** → Normal.
- **TSH 5–9.9 mU/L** → Repeat screening in ~1 week.
- **TSH 10–19.9 mU/L** → Serum confirmatory testing required.
- **TSH ≥ 20 mU/L** → Urgent referral + treatment.

## PKU
- **DBS (Stage 1)**: Phe < 4 mg/dL → Normal; if ≥ 4 → Do **HPLC** (no diet change before test).
- **HPLC (Stage 2)**: 4–6.9 → Observe (no diet change) • 7–9.9 → Repeat in 1 week • ≥10 → Treat + educate family.

### Special groups
Preterm, LBW/VLBW, macrosomia, NICU, transfusion, interfering meds, dialysis → follow protocol; the app shows notes.
